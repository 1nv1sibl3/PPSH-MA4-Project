Project overview

Research-rich storytelling grounded in student curiosity.

Welcome to our website, a virtual portal that takes you on an enthralling journey through the heart of Maharashtra and Sikkim. Here, art, writing, and civic data illustrate two distinct yet interconnected narratives.

Maharashtra

A western powerhouse filled with bustling cities, serene beaches, architectural marvels, and an ever-evolving arts scene. Dive into its layered history, governance milestones, culinary icons, and sporting spirit.

Sikkim

A Himalayan sanctuary stitched together with glacial lakes, alpine forests, and resilient cultures. Meet the monasteries, festivals, and communities safeguarding nature and heritage in equal measure.
