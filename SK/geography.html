Sikkim - Geography

Curated notes, maps, and stories gathered by our team.

Introduction

Sikkim is situated in the Himalayan mountains. It is mostly hilly with an elevation ranging from 280 metres to 8,586 metres. World’s third-largest peak, Kangchenjunga, lies on the border between Sikkim and Nepal. Its summit lies at 8586 m. There are a total of 28 peaks in Sikkim among which Hanuman Tok, Namchi, etc, are visited by many tourists, 80 glaciers, 227 high-altitude lakes like Gurudongmar, Tsongmo, Tsomgo Chho, Kathok Lake are some of the few highest lakes in the world, 5 major hot springs and eight mountain passes. Agriculture is not possible as there is not much plain surface available, However, some hills are cut down into terraces for terrace farming. There are more than 100 rivers and streams. Teesta, Rangeet, Lachen,etc are few of the many rivers which flow through Sikkim.

Climate

The hills of Sikkim mainly consist of gneiss and schist which weather to produce generally poor and shallow brown clay soils. The soil is coarse, with large concentrations of iron oxide. This type of soil tends to support evergreen and deciduous forest. The state has five seasons: winter, summer, spring, autumn, and monsoon season. Sikkim's climate ranges from subtropical in the south to tundra in the north.It is one of the few states in India to receive snowfall regularly. The average annual temperature for most of Sikkim is around 18 °C.In winters, because of the high altitude, temperature in the mountains can get as low as -40 degree celsius.The record for the longest period of continuous rain in Sikkim is 11 days

Flora and Fauna

Sikkim is situated in an ecological hotspot of the lower Himalayas, one of only three among the ecoregions of India. Sikkim is home to around 5000 species of flowering plants which 515 rare orchids, 60 primula species, 36 rhododendron species, 11 oak varieties, 23 bamboo varieties, 16 conifer species, 362 types of ferns and ferns allies, 8 tree ferns, and over 900 medicinal plants.The Noble Dendrobium is the official flower of Sikkim, while the rhododendron is the state tree.Sikkim is so diversified in terms of flora and fauna.The fauna of Sikkim include the snow leopard, musk deer, Himalayan tahr, red panda, Himalayan marmot, Himalayan serow, Himalayan goral, muntjac, common langur, Asian black bear,etc.Sikkim has more than 550 species of birds.Some of the most understudied species are Sikkimese arthropods, specifically butterflies.Khangchendzonga National Park and Pangolakha Wildlife Sanctuary are two examples of national park and widlife sanctuaries set up in state of Sikkim.
