GOVERNANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

Sikkim has a parliamentary system of representative democracy.Sikkim has a unicameral legislature, the Sikkim Legislative Assembly.Its state assembly has 32 seats

The judiciary consists of the Sikkim High Court and a system of lower courts. The High Court, located at Gangtok, has a Chief Justice along with two permanent justices. The Sikkim High Court is the smallest state high court in the country. Indian National Congress, Sikkim Sangram Parishad Party, Sikkim Krantikari Morcha party are some of the political parties popular in Sikkim.CM of Sikkim is Prem Singh Tamang and the current governor of Sikkim is Lakshman Acharaya.

DEMOGRAPHICS

Sikkim is India’s least populous state with 610,577 inhabitants(2011 Census).Though it is less populated state but it has a high population growth rate, averaging 12.36% percent between 2001 and 2011.The sex ratio is 889 females per 1,000 males, with a total of 321,661 males and 286,027 females

RELIGION

57.8% follow Hinduism, making it the state's majority religion.Between 2001 and 2011, Christianity was the fastest growing religion in Sikkim. Buddhism is followed by 27.4 percent of the population.
