Introduction

Maharashtra is the third-largest state in India with a total area of 307,713 km2. It is located in the north-western part of the Deccan Plateau. The state lies between 15°35' N to 22°02' N latitude and 72°36' E to 80°54' E longitude. Mumbai is the capital city of Maharashtra. Maharashtra is rich in black soil formed from weathering of rocks from volcanic Deccan Plateau. Black soil is suitable for cultivation of cotton because of its clayey nature. Fruits like mangoes, oranges, strawberries,etc , crops like wheat, rice and some pulses are cultivated.Maharashtra is the major producer of oilseeds, sugarcane , cotton and turmeric. The main rivers of the state are the Krishna, and its tributary, Bhima, the Godavari, and its main tributaries, Manjara, and Wardha-Wainganga and the Tapi, and its tributary Purna.They all are seasonal rivers and depend on rainwater. Maharashtra is divided into five geographic regions - Konkan in the western coastal region, Khandesh in the central part of the state, Marathwada is located in the southern part and Vidarbha in the easternmost part of the state. Neighbouring states of Maharashtra - Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Karnataka and Telangana

Climate

Maharashtra mostly experiences tropical wet and dry climate throughout the year. May is usually the warmest and January is the coldest. Monsoon annual rainfall of more than 100 cm is confined to the coastal region and adjoining Ghats region. Nagpur, Wardha and Chandrapur are some cities from Maharashtra which come under the list of few hottest cities.In winter, a cool dry spell occurs, with clear skies, gentle air breeze, and pleasant weather that prevails from October to February.

Flora and Fauna

Maharashtra has a variety of flora and fauna. It represented its diverse flora and fauna in the 2022 Republic Day.Maharashtra is ranked second among Indian states in terms of the recorded forest area.One of the many important floral species in Maharashtra is Bentinckia condapanna, also known as the hill areca nut. This is endemic and only found in the western ghats of India.Many trees like Red Silk Cotton, Ghost Tree, Indian Coral Tree, Shemat, Toddy Palm, Fig, Ber, Teak, Charcoal Tree, Banyan, Peepal Desi Badam, Bhokar, Bamboo, etc are found in the western ghats. Animal species present in the state are wild pigs, tiger, leopard, gaur, sloth bear, sambar, four-horned antelope, chital, barking deer, mouse deer, small Indian civet, golden jackal, jungle cat, and hare.The state provides legal protection to its tiger population through six dedicated tiger reserves under the precincts of the National Tiger Conservation Authority. Tadoba Andhari Tiger Reserve, Pench National Park, Sanjay Gandhi National Park, etc, are some of the best National Parks in Maharashtra.
