Education Infrastructure

Maharashtra is one of the top destinations for education in India with numerous school colleges and other educational institutions operating in the state maharashtra is flocked by students from all over country for pursuing a career in their own chosen field. Pune is a hub for popular colleges and institutions in Maharashtra. Other cities in Maharashtra that are known for having a sound educational base include Ahmednagar Kolhapur Aurangabad Nagpur and Mumbai. According to the statistics of census 2011 the literacy rate in the state of Maharashtra is 82.9 the male literacy rate is 89.82% and the female literacy rate is 75.48%.

The education system in Maharashtra has undergone a massive change over the last decade the courses and curriculum are made student friendly and economical the students healing from all backgrounds. In the year 2011 the criteria for selecting candidates for admission into colleges with decided to be best of five by Maharashtra State Board of Secondary Education. This means a student appearing in 6 subjects can choose marks of any five subjects in which he has scored the highest and apply for admission into colleges. However the students cannot Leave out the marks obtained for compulsory subjects Marathi or English. In 2010 school education system also under went a change full education could be made contemporary and relevant during same.

Education policies of Maharashtra

Sakshar Bharat

  • To impart functional literacy and numeracy to non-literate and non-numerate adults.
  • To enable new literate adults to continue their learning beyond basic literacy and acquire equivalency to the formal educational system.
  • To impart non-neo-literate relevant skill development programs to improve their earning and living conditions.
  • To promote a learning society by providing opportunities to neo-literate adults for continuing their education.

Infrastructure Development in Minority Institutes (IDMI)

  • The scheme would facilitate education of minorities by augmenting and strengthening school infrastructure in order to expand facilities for formal education to children of minority communities.
  • The scheme will encourage education facilities for girl children with special need and those who deprived educationally amongst minorities.
