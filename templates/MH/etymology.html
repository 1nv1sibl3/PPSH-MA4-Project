Etymology

The modern Marathi language of Maharashtra evolved from the “Maharashtri Prakrit”, and the word “Marhatta” is found in the Jain Maharashtrian literature. The most widely accepted theory among the linguistic scholars is that the words “Maratha” and “Maharashtra” ultimately derived from the combination of “Maha” and “Rashtrika” (the name of a tribe or dynasty of chiefs ruling in the Deccan region). An alternate theory states that the term is derived from “Maha” meaning “great” and “ratha/rathi” meaning “chariot/charioteer”, which refers to a skilful fighting force.
