GOVERNANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

Maharashtra is governed through a parliamentary system of representative democracy.It has bicameral state legislature where both Vidhan Sabha and Vidhan Parishad is there. The government of Maharashtra is a democratically elected body in India with the Governor as its constitutional head who is appointed by the President of India for a five-year term.Chief minister is the leader of the party or from the coalition of ministers.CM and his council runs day-to-day government functions while Governor is just the ceremonial head of the state.

For the purpose of administration, the state is divided into 6 divisions and 36 districts. Divisional Commissioner, an IAS officer is the head of administration at the divisional level. The administration in each district is headed by a District Magistrate, who is an IAS officer and is assisted by several officers belonging to state services. The judiciary in the state consists of Maharashtra high court, district,session and local courts.

Maharashtra’s politics is dominated by four political parties - Bharatiya Janata Party, Indian National Congress, Nationalist Congress Party and Shivsena. Apart from controlling the government, political families also control cooperative institutions, mainly cooperative sugar factories and district cooperative banks in the state. Bal Thackeray, Yashwantrao Chavan, Devendra Fadnavis, Uddhav Thackeray, Sharad Pawar are some important leaders who dominate the politics of Maharashtra.Current CM of Maharashtra is Eknath Shinde and Governor of Maharashtra is Ramesh Bais.

DEMOGRAPHICS

Maharashtra is the second-most populous state in India with a population of 112,374,333 (2011 Census). Contributing to 9.28 per cent of India's population, males and females are 58,243,056 and 54,131,277, respectively.The total population growth in 2011 was 15.99 per cent while in the previous decade it was 22.57 per cent.The state also includes migrants coming from various parts of India mostly from Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat and Karnataka.The 2011 census reported the human sex ratio is 929 females per 1000 males, which were below the national average of 943.

RELIGION

Hinduism is the major religion in Maharashtra(2011 Census).79.8 percent of the total population follow hinduism.Other prominent religion with a total population of 11.5 percent is Islam. Maharashtra has the highest number of buddhists in India. Marathi Buddhists account for 77.36% of all Buddhists in India. 5000 Jews and 44,000 Parsis reside in Mumbai.
